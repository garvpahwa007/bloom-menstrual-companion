# Fertile-window feature: source review

The cycle calendar can only offer a calendar-based estimate; it cannot establish that ovulation happened. ACOG says pregnancy may occur from sex five days before ovulation through one day after, and notes that ovulation in an average 28-day cycle is about 14 days before the next period. ACOG also describes fertility-awareness methods as methods that require specific, consistent use when used to prevent pregnancy; a calendar estimate alone is not that method. ACOG’s FAQ was last reviewed in February 2025. [1]

The NHS says the exact timing of ovulation is difficult to identify. It describes ovulation as occurring around 10–16 days before the next period for most women, and warns that the likely day-14 timing in a regular 28-day cycle does not apply to shorter or longer cycles. The NHS page was last reviewed on 5 January 2023 and listed its next review as 5 January 2026, which has passed as of this review date. [2]

ASRM defines the fertile window for counseling as the six-day interval ending on ovulation, and notes that its timing can vary considerably even among people who describe their cycles as regular. ACOG’s patient explanation includes the day after ovulation as a possible pregnancy day; Saheli therefore uses a conservative display range from five days before through one day after its estimated ovulation range, while emphasizing that the dates are uncertain. The app does not claim that ovulation occurred and does not present the estimate as contraception advice. [1] [3]

The two patient/professional sources use slightly different boundaries for the “fertile window.” Saheli’s displayed range intentionally includes the additional day ACOG identifies as a possible pregnancy day. This conservative calendar projection still cannot identify actual ovulation or substitute for a validated fertility-awareness method. A qualified clinician should review the feature before it is relied on beyond an educational prototype; native-Hindi medical wording should also receive human review.
